# Place

## What you see

The page of the place you chose. At the top is its name. Below that:

- "Location": for a point, "Latitude", "Longitude" and a "Geo URI". For anything else, the "Shape" and the note "This place is not a single point; its shape is kept as it is."
- "About": the "Description", if there is one.

Above the page are the buttons "Edit" and "Delete". If nothing is chosen the page says "Nobody selected." If the place was removed meanwhile it says "This place no longer exists."

## What you can do

1. Press "Edit" to change the place. See [Edit place](edit-place.md).
2. Press "Delete" to remove it. See [Delete place](delete-place.md).
3. Choose another place in the [list](places.md) to read it.

## Keyboard

None of its own.

## Not there yet

- You cannot see the place on a map.
- The window cannot draw or change the shape of a line or an area. It can rename and describe it.
