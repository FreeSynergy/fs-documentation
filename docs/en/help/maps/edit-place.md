# Edit place

## What you see

A form on the right with the fields "Name", "Latitude", "Longitude" and "Description". For a place that is not a single point, the two coordinate fields are missing. The buttons are "Save" and "Cancel".

## What you can do

To change a place:

1. Choose it in the [list](places.md) and press "Edit".
2. Change the fields.
3. Press "Save". You see "Saved." If you changed nothing it says "Nothing was changed."

To make a new place:

1. Press "New" above the list. An empty form opens.
2. Fill in the name and the coordinates.
3. Press "Save". You see "Created."

If something is wrong, Save does not go through and says why:

- "A place needs a name."
- "The latitude is a number from -90 to 90, like 52.52."
- "The longitude is a number from -180 to 180, like 13.405."

To give up:

1. Press "Cancel". If you changed something, it asks "You have unsaved changes. Keep editing, or discard them."
2. Press "Keep editing" or "Discard changes".

While you have unsaved changes, choosing another place says "Save or cancel your changes first." You can still search.

## Keyboard

Tab walks through the fields in reading order, then "Save", then "Cancel".
