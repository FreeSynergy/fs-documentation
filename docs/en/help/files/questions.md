# Questions

## What you see

A band at the bottom of the window. It appears when the window needs a word or an answer from you. It stands at the bottom on purpose, so the second press of something destructive is never under the first.

## What you can do

Typing a name or path:

1. Press "New folder" or "Rename". The band asks "Name of the new folder" or "New name of ...".
2. Type the name.
3. Press "Apply". Press "Cancel" to give up. If you typed something and press "Cancel", it asks "You have typed something. Discard it?". Choose "Keep typing" or "Discard what I typed".
4. If the name is not allowed, a line says "This name cannot be used".

Confirming:

1. After Delete, Delete forever, Delete folder or Empty trash, the band asks a question.
2. Press the "Yes, ..." button to do it, or "Keep it" to leave things as they are. The way out stands first.

A name clash:

1. If the name is taken, the band says so and "Nothing was changed."
2. Press "Keep both" (the incoming one gets a free name; nothing is replaced) or "Back".

Carrying:

1. After Move, Copy or Restore to, the band says what you are carrying.
2. Open the target folder and press "Move here", "Copy here" or "Restore here". Press "Cancel" to stop.

## Keyboard
