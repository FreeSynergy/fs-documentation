# Person

## What you see

The card of the person you picked. At the top stand marks, if they apply: "AI" for a participant that is an AI, and "Sensitive" with its categories for a sensitive card. Below, the card is shown in sections. Empty ones are left out:

- **Contact**: e-mail addresses, phone numbers and links.
- **Organisation**: organisations and titles.
- **Addresses**.
- **About**: kind, nickname and notes.
- **Roles**: the roles this person has.
- **Kept on the card**: the names of things the card holds that the window does not show. They are not lost.

If no one is picked, the area says "Nobody selected."

## What you can do

Edit the card:

1. Press **Edit**. See [Edit person](edit-person.md).

Delete the card:

1. Press **Delete**. The window asks and says it cannot be taken back here.
2. Press **Really delete** to go on, or **Keep** to stop.

## Keyboard

The window code defines no shortcuts for this view.
