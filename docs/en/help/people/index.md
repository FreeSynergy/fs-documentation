# People help

FreeSynergy People holds the people in your life. Each person is one card, and a card can carry several roles at once. Cards follow an open standard (JSContact), and you can bring contacts in and take them out as vCard. Cards you mark as sensitive stay on your device unless you release them by name.

## Views

- [People list](people-list.md): everyone, with search.
- [Person](person.md): one card, to read, edit or delete.
- [Edit person](edit-person.md): the form for a new person or an existing one.

## How to start

1. Open the program with `fs-people`. If no people server is running, the program starts one for you.
2. Press **New** to add the first person.
3. Type a name and press **Save**.

If you want your people to stay inside the one window and not be shared, start with `fs-people --local`.
