# People list

## What you see

A search field ("Search people") and the button **New** at the top. Below them a list of your people with the columns **Name** and **E-mail**. Next to the list, or below it in a narrow window, you see the person you picked. With nobody saved, the list says "Nobody here yet."

## What you can do

Find someone:

1. Click the search field.
2. Type a name, nickname, e-mail address, phone number or organisation.
3. If nobody matches, the list says "Nobody found for" and your words. Clear the field to see everyone.

Open a card:

1. Click a row. The card appears beside the list. See [Person](person.md).

See more people:

1. If the list is long, press **Show more** under the last row.

Add a person:

1. Press **New**. See [Edit person](edit-person.md).

## Keyboard

The window code defines no shortcuts for this view.

## Not there yet

- You cannot sort the list in the window.
- Groups cannot be managed here. A group card is listed and can be read.
- Importing and exporting vCard files is not in the window. You start it when you open the program, with `--import` and `--export`.
- The list does not update when another window changes a person.
