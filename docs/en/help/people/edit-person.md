# Edit person

## What you see

A form with the groups **Name**, **E-mail**, **Phone** and **Organisation**. A new person starts with one empty field in each group. Under a group that can grow, a button **Add: E-mail**, **Add: Phone** or **Add: Organisation** gives you one more field. At the bottom are **Save** and **Cancel**. The e-mail field shows the hint name@example.org, and the phone field shows +49 30 1234567.

## What you can do

Add a person:

1. Press **New** in the [People list](people-list.md).
2. Type the name and fill in what you know.
3. Press **Save**. The window says "Created."

Change a person:

1. Pick the person and press **Edit**.
2. Change the fields, or press an **Add** button for another address, number or organisation.
3. Press **Save**. The window says "Saved." Everything on the card that the form does not show stays as it was.

E-mail and phone are checked as you type, and the problem appears under the field. An e-mail needs an @ with text on both sides and a domain with a dot. A phone number starts with + and the country code, and has 7 to 15 digits. A field with a problem stops **Save** until you correct it.

Stop:

1. Press **Cancel**. If you changed something, the window asks. Press **Discard changes** to throw it away, or **Keep editing** to go on.

If someone else changed the card while you were editing, the window says so and keeps your text. Press **Save** again to write it anyway.

## Keyboard

The window code defines no shortcuts for this view.
