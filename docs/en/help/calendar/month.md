# Month

## What you see

a grid of the days of the month, with your events on it. The toolbar has **Today**, **<**, **>**, and the buttons **Month**, **Week**, **Day**, **Agenda** and **New**. The title shows the month and year. The same choices are in the menu bar under **View** and **Go**. Events in another time zone are shown in the zone the window uses. An event marked sensitive carries its mark. Tasks that are due appear at their due time, marked as a task.

## What you can do

Move in time:

1. Press **>** for the next period, **<** for the previous one, or **Today** to come back.
2. The menu bar has the same under **Go**: **Next**, **Previous**, **Today**.

Change the view:

1. Press **Month**, **Week**, **Day** or **Agenda**, or choose it under **View**.

Read an event:

1. Click it. See [Event](event.md). A click on a task opens it for reading; it is changed in the Tasks program.

Add an event:

1. Press **New**, or choose **Event**, then **New**. See [Edit event](edit-event.md).

## Keyboard

The window code defines no shortcuts for this view.
