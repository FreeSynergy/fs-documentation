# List

## What you see

A search field and the buttons **List**, **Status** and **New** along the top. Below them, your tasks in rows with the columns **Title**, **Due**, **Priority** and **Status**. The button of the view you are in is highlighted. Next to the list, or below it in a narrow window, you see the task you picked. If you have no tasks yet, the list says "No tasks yet."

A task marked sensitive shows its mark in its row.

## What you can do

Find a task:

1. Click the search field.
2. Type a word from the title or description. The list narrows as you type.
3. If nothing matches, the list says "Nothing found for" and your words. Clear the field to see all tasks again.

Open a task:

1. Click its row. The task appears beside the list. See [Task](task.md).

See more tasks:

1. If the list is long, press **Show more** under the last row.

Start a new task:

1. Press **New**, or choose **Task**, then **New** in the menu bar. See [Edit task](edit-task.md).

Switch the view:

1. Press **Status**, or choose **View**, then **Status**. See [Status](status.md).

## Keyboard

- Up and Down arrows, Page Up, Page Down, Home and End move the highlight through the rows.
- Enter or Space opens the highlighted row.
- On **Show more**, Enter or Space presses it.

## Not there yet

- "My next tasks" shows every open task, because there is no sign-in yet.
- You cannot sort the list or filter it by date, label or status in the window.
- Importing and exporting iCalendar files is not in the window. You start it when you open the program, with `--import` and `--export`.
