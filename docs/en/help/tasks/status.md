# Status

## What you see

One column for each status: To do, In progress, Done, Failed and Cancelled. Each task is a card in the column of its status. Above the columns, a warning tells you what moving a card does: it changes the status of the task itself, for everyone. A status that is worked out from the participants becomes fixed when you move the task, and it cannot go back to that here. A column without cards says "Empty". If a column has more cards than fit, it says how many are above or below.

This is not a board. Your own columns, swimlanes and limits do not exist yet.

## What you can do

Move a task with its buttons:

1. Click a card. The task opens beside the columns.
2. Under "Move to", press the button of the status you want.
3. The window tells you the task now has that status, for everyone.

Move a task with the keyboard:

1. Walk to a card with the arrow keys.
2. Press Space or Enter to pick it up.
3. Move to another column with the arrow keys.
4. Press Space or Enter to put it down. Escape gives up and leaves the card where it was.

Open a card from the keyboard:

1. Walk to the card with the arrow keys.
2. Press **Open**.

Search and switch view work as in the [List](list.md).

## Keyboard

- Tab goes to the columns.
- Arrow keys, Home, End, Page Up and Page Down walk over the cards.
- Space or Enter picks a card up and puts it down.
- Escape gives up a picked-up card.

## Not there yet

- Putting a card in another place in the same column does nothing. The window says the order within a column is not kept.
- A task that repeats cannot be moved here, because its status belongs to each occurrence.
- You cannot add columns of your own.
