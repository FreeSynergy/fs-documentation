# Search results

## What you see

When you type into the search field ("Search pages"), the tree is replaced by the hits. Each hit shows the title of the page and its path. If nothing matches, it says "Nothing found for" and your words. If the search fails, you see "The search failed" and why.

## What you can do

1. Click into the search field at the top left and type a word.
2. Click a hit to read the page. See [Page](page.md).
3. Clear the field to get the [Page tree](page-tree.md) back.

## Keyboard

None of its own.

## Not there yet

- Comments hidden in a page, such as HTML comments, are not searched.
