# Page tree

## What you see

The left side of the window, under the search field. It shows the pages as a tree of folders and pages. The page you are reading is highlighted. If the import found problems, a page called "(import problems)" is listed too.

If the list cannot be read, a line says "The list failed" and why. If there is nothing, it says "Nothing to show."

## What you can do

1. Click a folder to open or close it.
2. Click a page to read it. See [Page](page.md).
3. If you have unsaved changes in an edit, you first have to save or cancel them. The window says "Save or cancel your changes first."

## Keyboard

Enter and Space choose the entry under the cursor.

## Not there yet

- No moving, renaming or deleting of pages in the window.
- No collections or shelves.
- Titles with characters no installed font has can show as boxes. The window then tells you which characters and suggests installing a font such as Noto Sans.
