# Page

## What you see

The right side of the window. A bar on top has the button "Back", the path of the page, and the buttons "Edit" and "Export" (Export only when the app was started with `--export`). Under the bar is the page itself, with headings, lists, quotes, code, tables, bold, italic and links. Links are underlined. If nothing is open it says "Nothing to show." If a page cannot be read, you see "This could not be read" and why.

Notices appear under the bar, for example "Saved." or a note about characters that cannot be drawn.

## What you can do

1. To read another page, click it in the [Page tree](page-tree.md) or a [hit](search-results.md).
2. To follow a link, click it. If it leads nowhere you see: There is nothing at the place the link meant.
3. To go back, press "Back".
4. To change the page, press "Edit". See [Edit](edit.md).
5. To write the page to a file, press "Export". See [Export](export.md).

## Keyboard

Alt+Left goes back.
