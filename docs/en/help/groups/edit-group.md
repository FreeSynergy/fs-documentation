# Edit group

## What you see

A form on the right with the fields "Name" and "Kind". The buttons are "Save" and "Cancel".

## What you can do

To change a group:

1. Choose it in the [list](groups.md) and press "Edit".
2. Change the name or the kind.
3. Press "Save". You see "Saved." If you changed nothing it says "Nothing was changed."

To make a new group:

1. Press "New" above the list. An empty form opens.
2. Fill in the name and the kind.
3. Press "Save". You see "Created."

If the name is empty, Save does not go through and says "A group needs a name."

To give up:

1. Press "Cancel". If you changed something, it asks "You have unsaved changes. Keep editing, or discard them."
2. Press "Keep editing" or "Discard changes".

While you have unsaved changes, choosing another group says "Save or cancel your changes first." You can still search.

## Keyboard

Tab walks through the fields in reading order, then "Save", then "Cancel".

## Not there yet

- A change made by someone else is not noticed when you save. A group has no revision on the wire.
- Closing the window loses an unsaved edit.
