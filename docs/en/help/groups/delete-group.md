# Delete group

## What you see

When you press "Delete" on the [page of a group](group.md), the window asks "Delete ...? This cannot be taken back here." The "Delete" button turns into "Keep". A button "Really delete" appears on a line of its own below.

## What you can do

1. Choose the group and press "Delete".
2. Press "Really delete" to remove it. The window says that the group was deleted and shows the next one.
3. Press "Keep" if you changed your mind.

## Keyboard

None of its own.
