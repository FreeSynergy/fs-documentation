# Groups

## What you see

The left side of the window. At the top is a search field ("Search groups") and the button "New". Below it is the list with two columns, "Name" and "Kind". On a window narrower than 560 pixels the list stands above the page of the group.

If there are no groups it says "Nobody here yet." If your search finds none it says "Nobody found for" and your words. If the list cannot be read, a line says "The list failed" and why.

## What you can do

1. To find a group, type part of its name or its kind into "Search groups".
2. To look at a group, click its row. Its [page](group.md) opens on the right.
3. To make a group, press "New". See [Edit group](edit-group.md).
4. To see more, press "Show more" under the list. Tab to it and press Enter or Space if you prefer the keyboard.

## Keyboard

Enter and Space on "Show more" work like a click. The list itself has no arrow-key movement yet.
