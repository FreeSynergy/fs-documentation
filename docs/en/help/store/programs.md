# Programs

This is the page you see when the Store opens. It shows one card for every program.

## What you see

- A button **Refresh** at the top left.
- A grid of cards. Each card has a logo (or a coloured square with the first letter of the name), the name and the version. A program that is new in the list is marked **New**.
- At the bottom of each card: **Install** if the program is not on your machine, **Open** if it is, and **Open** next to **Update** if a newer version is offered. Next to the buttons are three dots, one above the other.
- A line "N update(s) found. Press Update on the cards." above the cards when updates are known. The number also shows in the window title.
- When you click a card's logo or name, a description panel opens beside the cards. It shows the name, the version and the description. For an installed program it also shows a part called "On this machine": how it was installed, its commands, its folder and its source.
- If there is nothing to show, you see "No programs yet. Switch on or add a source in the Store menu."

## What you can do

1. Install a program: press **Install** on its card. The [progress bar](installing.md) appears at the top.
2. Update a program: press **Update** on its card.
3. Start a program: press **Open** on its card.
4. Read about a program: click its card, or open the three dots and choose **Show description**. Press **Close** in the panel when you are done.
5. Visit its project page: open the three dots and choose **Go to the Git repository**.
6. Uninstall a program: open the three dots and choose **Uninstall**. The card asks "Uninstall (name)? Your data stays." Press **Uninstall** to confirm or **Cancel** to keep it. While it works the card says "Uninstalling...".
7. Read the sources again: press **Refresh**.

While a job runs, **Install**, **Update** and **Refresh** are switched off. **Uninstall** is not offered for a program that is not installed.

If something fails, a message appears at the top of the page. For example, a program that is running is not replaced. Close it and try again.

## Keyboard

- **Ctrl+Q** quits the Store. While a job runs, the Store refuses and tells you which program is being installed.
