# Settings

Here you choose how the Store looks for updates and what it does with them. You open this page with **Store**, then **Settings**. A click on a choice is saved at once.

## What you see

- **Look for updates**: **Off**, **At start**, **Daily** or **Hourly**. The one you chose stands out.
- **Install updates**: **Ask me** or **Automatically**. A hint says that Automatically only applies to programs that have automatic updates switched on.
- **Tell me (notification, badge)**: **On** or **Off**.
- A line "Last look for updates: ..." with the time, or "never", and a button **Look now**.
- **Look for updates every day, even when the window is closed**: a line saying whether the daily timer is on or off, and the buttons **Switch timer on** and **Switch timer off**.
- **Update these programs by themselves**: each installed program with **On** and **Off**. If nothing is installed you see "No program is installed yet."
- Below that, the list of [Sources](sources.md).

## What you can do

1. Pick how often to look for updates in **Look for updates**.
2. Pick **Ask me** or **Automatically** in **Install updates**.
3. Switch notifications on or off.
4. Press **Look now** to check at once. The page says "Looking for updates now. This can take a moment." and then "Done looking for updates." or why it did not work. The button is off while a check runs.
5. Press **Switch timer on** so the check also runs when the window is closed. If the timer cannot be switched, the page tells you why. The timer needs the choice Daily or Hourly.
6. Under **Update these programs by themselves**, set **On** for each program you want updated without asking.

## Keyboard

- **Ctrl+Q** quits the Store.
