# Bounce In

## Description

Bounce In brings an element into its final position with a springy motion: it
shoots past the target, springs back once or several times, and then comes to
rest. The character of the movement is playful and demanding of attention.

## Use

- land success messages and badges with a noticeable "set down" (e.g. a check
  mark)
- place stickers, emojis and gamified elements
- open modals in products with a playful character
- acknowledge additions to a cart with a little hop
- highlight onboarding elements
- let new entries arrive in lists and kanban boards

## Duration

- Typical: 400–700ms (including the springing back)
- Short variant for badges and icons: 300–400ms
- Do not set the spring-back too short, otherwise the spring feels hard

## Difference from Bounce Out

- Bounce In: on arrival the element crosses past the final position and springs
  to rest
- Bounce Out: the element briefly leans at the starting position before it
  leaves it and disappears
