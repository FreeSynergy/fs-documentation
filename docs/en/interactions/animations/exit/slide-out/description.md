# Slide Out

## Description

Slide Out makes an element glide out of the visible area in a straight line –
to the left, right, top or bottom. The space becomes free or gives room for
content moving up.

## Use

- slide drawers and sidebars out when closing them
- let bottom sheets glide back via the close button or a swipe
- push toasts out after their display time
- page change: the old page glides out opposite to the new one
- close menus and popovers in the direction of their menu axis
- slide completed list entries out of the list to the right

## Duration

- Typical: 200–350ms
- Exits are usually animated a bit shorter and faster than entrances
- Swipe-based removal: 150–200ms, follows the gesture

## Difference from Slide In

- Slide Out: the element leaves the view in the direction of the movement and
  disappears
- Slide In: the element comes from outside and ends at its layout position
