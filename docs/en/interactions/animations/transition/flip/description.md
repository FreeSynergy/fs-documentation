# Flip

## Description

Flip turns an element by 180° around a horizontal or vertical axis, showing its
back side meanwhile – like turning over a playing card. The change of content
happens at the 90° turning point, and perspective produces a spatial impression.

## Use

- turn vocabulary and learning cards over (question ↔ answer)
- credit card form: front ↔ back (CVV field)
- buttons swap front and back side on click
- dashboard tiles with extra information on the back
- show details of an element on the back of the card
- swap switch icons by turning them against the back side
- postcard and gallery effects on landing pages

## Duration

- Typical: 300–600ms
- Icon flip: 300ms, cards with text: 500–600ms

## Difference from Rotate

- Flip: a turn of 180° with a change of side – afterwards the back side is visible
- Rotate: a turn through any angle around its own axis, the front side stays
  visible
