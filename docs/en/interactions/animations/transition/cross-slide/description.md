# Cross Slide

## Description

Cross Slide swaps two elements in the same place by moving both sideways at the
same time: the old one leaves the surface in the direction of the movement, the
new one comes in from the opposite side. Both are briefly visible next to each
other, and the surface stays filled throughout. Direction and distance are
unambiguously readable – "one page further" or "one page back".

## Use

- switch tab contents, see [tabs](../../../../components/widgets/navigation/index.md)
- page the pages of an assistant forwards and back
- push a carousel and a calendar month by month
- switch between the monthly, weekly and daily calendar view
- change the sort order or the filter view sideways
- back and forward navigation with a directional reference

## Duration

- Typical: 200–350 ms
- Short distances (tabs): 200 ms
- Whole surfaces (pages, calendars): 300–350 ms

## Difference from Cross Fade and Slide In

- Cross Fade cross-fades two elements without motion and names no direction; Cross
  Slide moves both of them and therefore shows a sequence.
- Slide In brings a single element in from outside into a surface that was empty
  before; Cross Slide pushes a second element out at the same time.
