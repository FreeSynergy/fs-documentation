## It is about you, not the software

Most programs expect you to adapt: to their menus, their jargon, their price tiers. We turn that around.

At the start of every line of code there is a question: **What do you need this for?** Nobody wants a Kanban board. People want to know what to do next. Nobody wants a CRM. People want to remember those who matter to them.

That is why our programs are named for what they are. No fantasy names, no brands you have to learn first. You know at once what is inside. And because they all speak the same language, they work together in the background without any setup: an appointment knows its tasks, a task knows the people working on it, a message knows the document you are talking about.

## See it the way you want

If you like how the big programs look, take an interface that feels like what you know. If that is too much, take one with three buttons. If you need every detail, take the one that shows everything. **And if none fits, build your own**: with an AI at your side, describing what you want is enough.

That works because we build differently: a strong engine under every program, any number of interfaces on top. Switch the interface whenever you like. Your data stays where it is.

## Start small. Grow as far as you like.

- **Alone.** Your diary, your appointments, your shopping list. Local on your phone, no account, no cloud.
- **Two of you, a family, a club.** Share the calendar, plan the summer party, vote on who brings what. Your list becomes a shared one without starting over.
- **In a team.** Projects, tasks, schedules, a wiki for everything you know, a whiteboard for ideas without a shape yet.
- **In a company.** The same building blocks make what are otherwise expensive single products: groupware, project management, CRM, help desk, ERP, moderation and participation, media. As a web application for thousands, **with the same programs that run on your phone.**

## Free, no small print

No paywall, no "premium", no ads, not today and not later. Whoever has little money gets the same software as a corporation.

## Your data belongs to you

You work on your copy, and it is kept for you at all times. Nothing leaves your device unless you say so. Messages are encrypted. When you leave, you take everything with you, in open formats any other program can read.

## People first. AIs welcome, but recognisable

AIs may take part, as participants, not as secret listeners. Every AI is marked, so you know who you are talking to. It does not act without your approval. And if you do not want it, you lock it out.

## Open down to the last building block

We store everything in open standards. So you can move to another program with all your data at any time, also one that is not ours. Where there is no standard yet, we write one and make it free for everyone.

## FreeSynergy.Store

One place for everything. Get the Store, and from there install any program with one click: new interfaces, themes, extensions. It keeps everything up to date and checks every file before it reaches your device.

**We are building, and you can be part of it from the start.** The first programs are ready to try. What already runs and what is still to come is stated honestly in [the overall plan](programs/apps/roadmap.md).
