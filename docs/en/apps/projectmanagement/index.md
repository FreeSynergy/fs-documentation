## What is Project Management?

Project management is the process of planning, organizing, directing, and monitoring projects. Project management tools support:

- Manage tasks and milestones
- Create schedules and Gantt charts
- Assign team members and responsibilities
- Track progress and milestones
- Resource planning
- Collaboration and communication

## Application Areas

- **Software Development**: Agile and waterfall project management
- **Marketing**: Plan marketing campaigns
- **Product Management**: Product development
- **Construction**: Manage construction projects
- **Event Management**: Plan events and conferences
- **Consulting**: Manage consulting projects

## Benefits of Project Management Tools

- **Overview**: All projects and tasks in one place
- **Transparency**: Progress and status visible
- **Collaboration**: Team collaboration
- **Time savings**: Automation and workflow
- **Reporting**: Success control and analysis
- **Integration**: Connection with other tools

## Selection Criteria

When choosing a project management tool, consider the following criteria:

- **Features**: Which features are required?
- **Methodology**: Agile, waterfall, or hybrid methodologies?
- **Scalability**: How many projects and users?
- **Ease of use**: How user-friendly is it?
- **Integration**: How well does the system fit into the existing infrastructure?
- **Costs**: Free or paid?
