# CRM

## Overview

This section contains documentation for various CRM (Customer Relationship Management) systems.

## Available Systems

- **civicrm** - Constituent relationship management
- **suitecrm** - Open source CRM
- **pipedrive** - Sales pipeline CRM
- **salesforce** - Enterprise CRM platform
- **hubspot** - Inbound marketing and CRM platform
- **espocrm** - Open source CRM
- **zoho-crm** - CRM software

## Skin Documentation

Programmes in this category follow the wiki schema (seven files per programme: `index`, `menu`, `features`, `description`, `structure`, `shortcuts`, `api`) under [/en/apps/crm/skins/](/en/apps/crm/skins/); the detailed skin documents are translated there one by one.

## What is a CRM?

A CRM is the systematic management of customer relationships. CRM systems include:

- Managing contact data
- Tracking interactions and communication
- Sales pipelines and forecasting
- Marketing and service integration
