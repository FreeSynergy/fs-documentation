## What is a Help Desk?

A help desk is a system for managing customer inquiries and support tickets. Help desk systems include:

- Ticket creation and management
- Ticket categorization and prioritization
- SLA management and response times
- Knowledge base for frequently asked questions
- Customer portal for self-service
- Reporting and analytics

## Application Areas

- **Customer Support**: Manage customer service
- **IT Help Desk**: Internal IT support
- **Technical Support**: Technical support for products
- **Sales Support**: Support for sales team
- **Internal Support**: Support for employees

## Benefits of Help Desk Systems

- **Overview**: All inquiries in one place
- **Prioritization**: Prioritize important inquiries
- **SLA Management**: Maintain response times
- **Knowledge Base**: Document frequently asked questions
- **Reporting**: Success control and analysis
- **Integration**: Connection with other tools

## Selection Criteria

When choosing a help desk system, consider the following criteria:

- **Features**: Which features are required?
- **Scalability**: How many tickets per day?
- **Ease of use**: How user-friendly is it?
- **Integration**: How well does the system fit into the existing infrastructure?
- **Costs**: Free or paid?
- **Self-hosted or Cloud**: Hosting options
