# osTicket

> Category: [helpdesk](../../index.md)

## What it is

osTicket is the classic among open-source helpdesks: PHP/MySQL, e-mail ingest, help topics, filter rules and a plain agent and customer portal. No bells and whistles, but proven for decades and easy to host.
