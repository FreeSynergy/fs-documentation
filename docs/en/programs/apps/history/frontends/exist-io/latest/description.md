# Exist.io latest — Description

## Overview
A paid service that combines automatic data from connected services with manual logging and reports patterns.

## What it does
- Pulls data from health, fitness, productivity, music, calendar and weather sources (for example Apple Health, Health Connect, Garmin, Oura, Withings, Strava, RescueTime, Todoist, Toggl, GitHub, Last.fm, Spotify).
- Manual entries: mood, energy, stress, custom tags.
- Finds correlations, shows trends, sends a weekly summary email, daily insights.

## Who it suits
People who track themselves and want to see links between areas of life.

## Differentiation
### Exist.io vs. ActivityWatch
Exist is a hosted hub across many services; ActivityWatch is local and computer-only. Exist stores your data on the vendor's servers.

## Technical details
Apps for Android, iOS and web. Price per the product page: 6.99 USD per month or 62.90 USD per year, 30-day trial (may change). Data location and encryption: Open. The search results mention an unconfirmed claim that the product is being sold; treat as Open.
