# Google Contacts — Layout

## Open

The layout was not inspected in the live product and is not described in the help pages that were read. Known only from those pages:

- A menu at the top left contains the entry "Merge & fix".
- In the contact list a check box appears when hovering over a picture or monogram; selecting contacts shows actions such as merge at the top right.
- The contact page shows a "History" area.

Everything else (panels, columns, mobile layout) is Open.
