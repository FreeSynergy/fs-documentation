# Google Contacts — Description

## What the program does

Google Contacts keeps the address book of a Google Account. Entries are created by hand, imported, or collected automatically when you write to someone who is not yet stored. The same data appears in Gmail, on Android phones and in other services of the account.

## Differentiation

| Compared with | Difference |
|---------------|------------|
| Plain CardDAV address books | Duplicates are detected and offered for merging by the program. |
| Monica | No relationship history; Google Contacts is an address book, not a relationship diary. |

## Who it suits

People who live inside a Google Account and want contacts everywhere without setup. Not for those who need data on their own server.
