# Google Contacts — Features

## Documented in the help pages

- Create one or several contacts; they sync with the account and with Android devices.
- **Merge & fix**: suggestions of duplicates inside one account; accept one (Merge) or all (Merge all). Merging across different Google Accounts is not possible.
- Select contacts yourself and merge them with the merge icon.
- Split a merged contact again (separate article on undoing changes).
- **Other contacts**: addresses of people you wrote to are collected there; collecting can be switched off in Gmail settings. Not shown on iPhone, iPad or Android.
- **History** per contact: edits are recorded; older contacts may have none.

## Open

- Labels, import and export, sharing, "contact suggestions" other than duplicates: not covered by the pages read; not described here.
