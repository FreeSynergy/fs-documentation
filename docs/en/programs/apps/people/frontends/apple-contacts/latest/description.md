# Apple Contacts — Description

## What the program does

Contacts stores people, syncs them between a person's Apple devices and with added accounts, and lets the person share their own details. The iPhone guide lists these topics: add and use contact information, edit contacts, your own contact info, accounts, sharing with NameDrop, use contacts from the Phone app, merge or hide duplicates, sync, import, export.

## Differentiation

| Compared with | Difference |
|---------------|------------|
| Google Contacts | Sharing between nearby devices (NameDrop) instead of cloud suggestions; both offer duplicate merging. |
| Monica | An address book, not a relationship diary. |

## Who it suits

People who use Apple devices and want contacts without setup.
