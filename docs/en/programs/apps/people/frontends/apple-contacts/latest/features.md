# Apple Contacts — Features

## Documented

- **NameDrop**: bring two iPhones (or an iPhone and an Apple Watch) close together; the person picks what to share. Shared: name, the chosen phone number or e-mail address, contact poster. Not shared: home address, birthday and other card fields. Devices must be a few centimetres apart. Separating the devices or locking the phone cancels the transfer. A "Receive only" choice exists (secondary source). NameDrop only adds new contacts; it does not update an existing one (Apple Watch guide).
- Edit what NameDrop sends by editing the own card (for example share only initials).
- Merge or hide duplicate contacts; sync across devices; add or remove accounts; import and export.
