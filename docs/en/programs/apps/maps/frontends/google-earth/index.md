# Google Earth

> Category: [maps](../../index.md) · Type: 3D globe, map stories · Vendor: Google · Licence: proprietary

## What it is
Google Earth shows the planet as a 3D globe in the browser and in mobile apps. Besides looking around, you can measure, open ground-level imagery and build your own "projects" (maps with stories) and share them.

## Notable ideas
- 3D globe as the starting point, not a flat map. Why: a different mental model for place, scale and terrain.
- Guided tours (Voyager): curated stories that move the view from place to place. Why: a map can tell a story, not only answer a search.
- Projects shared through a file store (Google Drive). Why: own maps with narrative, shared like documents.
- Elevation profile along a drawn line (added April 2026).
