# Google Earth latest — Description

## Overview
A 3D globe with satellite and aerial imagery, 3D buildings and terrain, available in the browser (WebAssembly) and as mobile apps.

## What it does
- Fly across the globe, tilt to see 3D terrain and buildings.
- Measure distances, paths and polygon sizes; elevation profile along a drawn line.
- Open Street View imagery, including past dates in some places.
- Create projects (own maps and stories) and share them.
- Voyager: curated guided tours. Current scope: Open (the newest source found is the 2017 launch post).

## Who it suits
People who want to explore, teach or tell a story about places.

## Differentiation
### Google Earth vs. uMap / Felt
uMap and Felt start from a flat map to draw on; Earth starts from the globe and the view. Felt is built for working together on data.

## Technical details
Proprietary, hosted by the vendor. Browser version needs WebAssembly (two launch variants: multi-threaded and single-threaded, seen on the product page). Offline use, self-hosting, storage format of projects: Open.
