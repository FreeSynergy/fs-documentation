# Flathub

> Category: [store](../../index.md)

## What it is

Flathub is the main app store for Flatpak on Linux: a web catalogue plus a repository from which the Flatpak tools and software centres install apps in a sandbox.

## Notable ideas

- **Verified publishers**: a verified app shows a mark on its page; the developer proves control of the domain or code-hosting account behind the app ID.
- **Subsets for users**: a client can be limited to verified apps, to free software, or to both.
- **Stable and beta channels** as separate remotes.
- Listed here for the idea of proving who publishes an app (ADR 0013).
