# Restream

> Category: [streaming](../../index.md)

## What it is

Restream (restream.io) is a **cloud multistreaming service**: one encoder stream is accepted and passed on simultaneously to many platforms (YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok and others). Plus a browser studio, unified chat, events/scheduling and analytics. No self-hosting — everything runs through the web dashboard and its APIs.
