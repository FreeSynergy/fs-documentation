# Loomio

> Category: [surveys](../../index.md)

## What it is

Loomio is open-source software for group decision-making: share context, discuss, gather input, vote and keep a record of the outcome. It aims to replace scattered email and chat threads.

## Notable ideas

- Discussion and decision sit in one thread; the outcome stays attached to its reasoning.
- Built for cooperatives, nonprofits, boards, unions and distributed teams, so for one group at a time.
- Documented because it matches our group-first decisions (ADR 0013).
