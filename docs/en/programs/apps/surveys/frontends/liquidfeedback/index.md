# LiquidFeedback

> Category: [surveys](../../index.md)

## What it is

LiquidFeedback is open-source software for developing proposals and deciding online, published by the Public Software Group (Berlin) under the MIT licence. **Maintenance state: stagnant.**

## Notable ideas

- **Delegation**: a participant votes directly on topics they care about and passes their voice to someone else on the rest.
- **Preferential aggregation**: no predefined questions; participants develop alternatives and state preferences.
- Proposal development and decision are one process.
- Documented because delegation is the model for voting-weight transfer (ADR 0013).
