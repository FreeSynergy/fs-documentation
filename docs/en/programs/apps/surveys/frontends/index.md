# Surveys — Frontends

Frontends of well-known programs in this category, analysed for their **functions** — nothing is copied (ADR 0013). Our own program for this category is `FreeSynergy.Surveys`; its capabilities and status live in [requirements](../requirements.md).

## Programs

| Program | Versions | What it is |
|---------|----------|------------|
| [Consul Democracy](consul/index.md) | [latest](consul/latest/description.md) | Citizen participation for governments: debates, proposals, budgets, polls. |
| [Decidim](decidim/index.md) | [latest](decidim/latest/description.md) | Participatory spaces with configurable components. |
| [LiquidFeedback](liquidfeedback/index.md) | [latest](liquidfeedback/latest/description.md) | Proposals with delegated voting; development stagnant since about 2021. |
| [Loomio](loomio/index.md) | [latest](loomio/latest/description.md) | Group discussion and decision in one thread. |
| [Polis](polis/index.md) | [latest](polis/latest/description.md) | Maps the opinions of large groups. |
