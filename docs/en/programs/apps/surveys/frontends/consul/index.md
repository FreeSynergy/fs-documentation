# Consul Democracy

> Category: [surveys](../../index.md)

## What it is

Consul Democracy is open-source software for citizen participation by governments and organisations: debates, proposals, voting, participatory budgeting, collaborative legislation and polls.

## Notable ideas

- One site offers several participation modes side by side.
- Separate guides for administrators, citizens and developers, plus advanced guides on participatory budgeting and citizen proposals.
- Documented because it is built for public bodies at city scale (ADR 0013).
