# Grocy - Description

## What the program does

Grocy keeps track of what a household owns and what needs doing. Purchases are logged, the program tells when something is due or low, and it puts missing items on a shopping list. It also manages chores, a to-do list, equipment manuals and battery charge dates.

## Differentiation

| System | Difference |
|--------|------------|
| Snipe-IT | Snipe-IT tracks individual assets and who holds them; Grocy counts quantities of consumables. |
| ERP systems | No warehouses or accounting; household scale. |

## Technical details

- Web app, responsive, night mode; does not work offline (per the home page).
- Barcode lookup through Open Food Facts built in, other services via plugins.
- Language and licence: Open.

## Who it suits

Households and small groups that want shared stock and chores.
