# Resources — Frontends

Frontends of well-known programs in this category, analysed for their **functions** — nothing is copied (ADR 0013). Our own program for this category is `FreeSynergy.Resources`; its capabilities and status live in [requirements](../requirements.md).

| Program | Versions | What it is |
|---------|----------|------------|
| [ERPNext](erpnext/index.md) | [latest](erpnext/latest/description.md) | Business suite on the Frappe framework; DocTypes generate list and form views. |
| [Grocy](grocy/index.md) | [latest](grocy/latest/description.md) | Household stock, shopping list, recipes, chores. |
| [Odoo](odoo/index.md) | [latest](odoo/latest/description.md) | Modular business apps that build on each other. |
| [Snipe-IT](snipe-it/index.md) | [latest](snipe-it/latest/description.md) | IT asset management with check-out and check-in. |
