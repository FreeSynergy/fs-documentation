# Snipe-IT - Features

- **Assets**: valuable items with a unique tag; check-out to people, locations or other assets.
- **Accessories**: untagged interchangeable items, checked out to people or assets.
- **Components**: parts checked out to assets.
- **Consumables**: checked out to people only, never checked in.
- **Status labels**: four kinds, deciding assignability.
- Optional colour tags and show/hide of table columns.
- Licences, audit, reports: Open (not on the page read).
