# Spark — Description

## What the program does

Spark shows the mail of several accounts in one place, sorts priority mail from the rest, and lets a team work on mails together: private comments, shared drafts, a shared inbox with team roles.

## Differentiation

| Compared with | Difference |
|---------------|------------|
| Superhuman | Team functions around a mail are central. |
| Chatwoot, Zammad (helpdesk) | Spark stays an e-mail client; helpdesks add tickets and channels. |

## Who it suits

Small teams that answer mail together without a ticket system.
