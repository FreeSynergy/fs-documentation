# HEY

> Category: [messenger](../../index.md) · Type: e-mail · Vendor: Basecamp (not verified on the pages read) · Licence: proprietary ⚠️

## What it is

HEY is a hosted e-mail service with its own apps and web interface. Its address space is @hey.com, with custom domains and sending from external addresses possible. It changes the inbox itself instead of copying the usual folder model.

## Notable ideas

Why this program is documented here (ADR 0013):

- **The Screener**: a first-time sender is held back until the person decides whether they may write again. Relevant for us: consent before a stranger reaches the inbox, in line with people-first.
- **Imbox, The Feed, Paper Trail**: three places by kind of mail: people and services you care about, newsletters (already open, newest first), receipts and transactional mail.
- **Set Aside, Reply Later, Bubble Up**: piles for later and a way to send a mail away so it returns to the top.
- **Clips** (saved text snippets from mails) and **Collections** (several threads on one named, shareable page).
