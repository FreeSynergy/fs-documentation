# HEY — Description

## What the program does

HEY sorts incoming mail by what it is and by whether the sender is approved. A new sender lands in the Screener; once approved, mail goes to the Imbox (important), the Feed (newsletters) or Paper Trail (receipts). Mail can be set aside, kept for a later reply, or sent away to come back at a chosen time.

## Differentiation

| Compared with | Difference |
|---------------|------------|
| Superhuman | Superhuman speeds up a normal inbox; HEY changes what the inbox contains. |
| Apple Mail | Apple Mail classifies mail into categories; HEY asks the person per sender. |

## Who it suits

People who want fewer unwanted mails and accept a closed, hosted service.
