# Delta Chat — Description

## What the program does

Delta Chat gives the experience of a messenger on top of mail-like delivery. Contacts are added in person or by link; messages and attachments are end-to-end encrypted; relays store messages briefly while the recipient is offline and see only sender and receiver addresses (random by default) and message size.

## Differentiation

| Compared with | Difference |
|---------------|------------|
| Signal | Not tied to a phone number and not to one operator; relays can be chosen. |
| Element/Matrix | Uses mail-like delivery, not a federated room protocol. |

## Who it suits

People who want a private messenger that does not depend on a single provider.

## Open

Stated limits: no forward secrecy and no post-quantum protection yet; both planned with Autocrypt v2 (the page says 2026). Sealed Sender not supported yet.
