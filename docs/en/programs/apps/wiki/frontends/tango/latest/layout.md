# Tango: layout

Built from help-centre descriptions, not from a screenshot review. Region names are descriptive, not vendor labels, unless quoted.

## Surfaces

1. **Browser extension side panel.** Opened from the Tango icon in the top-right of Chrome or Edge. Shows starred, recently viewed and site-matching guides ([side panel](https://help.tango.ai/en/articles/7926363-how-do-i-use-the-tango-side-panel)). Also shows a number for guides available on the current page ([red number](https://help.tango.ai/en/articles/7885371-why-do-i-see-a-red-number-next-to-my-tango-extension)).
2. **Capture controller.** A panel on the left of the screen. Its controller sits bottom-left. Controls: Complete (green checkmark), Pause, Restart, Discard, Live Blur (Pro/Ent), voice transcription ([documentation how-to](https://help.tango.ai/en/articles/7966907-how-do-i-use-tango-for-documentation), [start capture](https://help.tango.ai/en/articles/5971654-how-do-i-start-capturing-a-workflow)).
3. **Guide editor.** Title, a table of contents of steps (drag to reorder), per-step title, description and image, image editor (arrows, boxes, text, blur on Pro/Ent). Each step has an "action box" (an orange box, per the help article title "Can I move the orange box?"; [article](https://help.tango.ai/en/articles/7198488-can-i-move-the-orange-box)). Its placement rules were not read.
4. **Share & Export.** Single entry point for sharing and exporting ([documentation how-to](https://help.tango.ai/en/articles/7966907-how-do-i-use-tango-for-documentation)).
5. **Team Library.** Lists guides of a Workspace. Supports multi-select and bulk action bar ([bulk actions](https://help.tango.ai/en/articles/16652169-how-do-i-use-bulk-actions-in-the-team-library)).
6. **Settings & members.** Admin area: members, roles, branding ([branding](https://help.tango.ai/en/articles/5971709-can-i-add-my-own-branding-to-tango-workflows)).
7. **Desktop app.** Separate app for capture outside the browser (Mac, Windows) ([desktop](https://help.tango.ai/en/collections/3462416-desktop)).

## Viewer layouts (shared guide)

- **Link:** scrollable vertical page.
- **Embed:** slideshow, clickable sideways or scrollable down ([documentation how-to](https://help.tango.ai/en/articles/7966907-how-do-i-use-tango-for-documentation)).
- **Guide Me:** the guide runs over the live app, step by step.
- **Export:** PDF, Markdown, HTML (Pro/Ent) ([pricing](https://www.tango.ai/pricing)).
