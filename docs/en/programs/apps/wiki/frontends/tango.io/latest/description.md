# Tango.io - Description

## Overview

Tango.io is a modern knowledge management platform focused on collaboration and document management.

## What Tango.io Does

Tango.io is a modern platform for knowledge management and collaboration, focused on simple operation, visual organization and strong integrations.

## Advantages

- **Modern UI**: Very user-friendly design
- **Block-based**: Intuitive block-based editing
- **Strong embeds**: 100+ embed support (YouTube, Figma, etc.)
- **Collaborative**: Real-time collaboration
- **Flexible**: Versatile document organization
- **Fast**: Fast loading and editing
- **Affordable**: Cheaper than Confluence & co.

## Distinction from Other Systems

### Tango.io vs. Notion
- **Notion** is more established with more features (databases, formulas)
- **Tango.io** is simpler and faster to use
- **Notion** has better databases, **Tango.io** has better embeds

### Tango.io vs. Confluence
- **Confluence** is enterprise-grade with high costs
- **Tango.io** is cheaper and simpler
- **Confluence** has better Jira integration, **Tango.io** has a more modern UI

### Tango.io vs. Wiki.js
- **Wiki.js** is open source with more customization
- **Tango.io** is commercial with support
- **Wiki.js** has more editor options, **Tango.io** has better embeds

## Technical Details

- **Backend**: Node.js
- **Database**: PostgreSQL
- **Frontend**: React
- **Authentication**: OAuth2, SSO
- **Hosting**: Cloud option (SaaS)

## Who Is Tango.io Suited For?

- **Teams**: For collaboration and knowledge management
- **Agile projects**: For fast writing and organization
- **Startups**: For cost-effective documentation
- **Design teams**: For visual organization and embeds
