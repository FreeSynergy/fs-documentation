## What is Marketing Automation?

Marketing automation refers to software solutions for automating marketing processes. They include:

- **Email marketing**: Manage and send campaigns
- **CRM**: Manage customer relationships
- **Analytics**: Analyze user behavior and conversions
- **Lead management**: Generate, qualify, and handle leads
- **Personalization**: Create customer-specific content
- **Integration**: Connect with other tools and platforms

## Benefits of Marketing Tools

- **Time savings**: Automate repetitive tasks
- **Customer proximity**: Personalized communication
- **Measurability**: Success control and analytics
- **Scalability**: Efficient growth
- **Integration**: Connection with other tools
- **ROI**: Better measurement of marketing success

## Application Areas

- **Email marketing**: Newsletters and campaigns
- **CRM**: Manage customer relationships
- **Lead generation**: Generate and qualify leads
- **Analytics**: Analyze user behavior
- **Personalization**: Customer-specific content
- **E-commerce**: Online shop integration

## Selection Criteria

When choosing a marketing tool, consider the following criteria:

- **Features**: Which features are required?
- **Scalability**: How many customers and contacts?
- **Integration**: How well does the system fit into the existing infrastructure?
- **Ease of use**: How user-friendly is it?
- **Costs**: Price-performance ratio
- **Data sovereignty**: Who owns the data?
