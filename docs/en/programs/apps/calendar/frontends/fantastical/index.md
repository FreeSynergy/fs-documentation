# Fantastical

> Category: [calendar](../../index.md) · [frontends](../index.md)

## What it is

Fantastical is a commercial calendar and task app from Flexibits for Apple platforms and Windows. It sits on top of existing calendar accounts and adds its own input and view features. ✅ (help index lists Mac, iOS, Windows.)

## Notable ideas

- **Plain-language input:** one typed sentence becomes an event or a task; date, time, place, calendar, alert, repetition and task priority are read from the text ✅. Why here: a quick-entry field is the fastest way to create an item (ADR 0013).
- **Calendar sets and views** exist as help topics ✅ (details Open).
