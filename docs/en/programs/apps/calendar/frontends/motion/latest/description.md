# Motion - Description

## What the program does

Combines a calendar, a task list and project management; an AI scheduler fills the calendar with tasks. External Google or Outlook calendars are read so busy times are respected ✅.

## Differentiation

- Against Reclaim: Motion is an own workspace with projects and users, not only a layer over a calendar. ⚠️
- Against Planner (GNOME): Planner schedules by dependencies, but by hand; Motion does it continuously.

## Who it suits

Individuals and small teams who prefer the tool to plan their day.
