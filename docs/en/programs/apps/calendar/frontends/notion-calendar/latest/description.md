# Notion Calendar - Description

## What the program does

A keyboard-oriented calendar front end over existing calendar accounts, with several time zones shown together and links that offer free slots to others. Commercial service. ✅

## Differentiation

- Against Cal.com: the scheduling link is a feature of a personal calendar, not a separate booking product.
- Against Google Calendar: time zones can be labelled and travelled to; the help states the zone is first taken from the Google account ✅.

## Who it suits

People who schedule across time zones. Which accounts besides Google are supported: Open.
