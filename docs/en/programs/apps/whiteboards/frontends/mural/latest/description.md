# Mural - Description

## What the program does

Mural structures moderation: besides the canvas it brings "sequences" — a series of moderated steps with time allocations that facilitators and participants go through together. It targets facilitation more strongly than free scribble-whiteboarding.

## Advantages

- **Moderation rhythm** as a product feature (timer, steps, breakouts)
- tidy UI with little tool distraction
- EU hosting option

## Differentiation

| System | Difference |
|--------|------------|
| **Miro** | larger app ecosystem and API; Mural is richer in the facilitation core |
| **FigJam** | close to design workflows (Figma), less depth in facilitation |
| **OpenBoard** | classroom/presentation tool, no team SaaS |

## Technical details

- **Model**: SaaS (workspaces; Mural.co), EU/US residency; desktop app/clients
- **Auth**: email/Google/Microsoft/SSO (SAML/SCIM Enterprise)
- **Pricing**: Free/Team/Business/Enterprise

## Who it suits

- Workshop and consulting teams that want to standardise their procedures
- FreeSynergy skin candidate for "guided interaction" (the sequence model)
