# Apple Shortcuts

> Category: [workflows](../../index.md)

## What it is

Apple Shortcuts is the automation app of Apple devices: a shortcut is a sequence of actions that work with apps, content and web services. Note: this is a personal-automation tool, not a suite; it is filed under Workflows because the map lists it there.

## Notable ideas

- **Everyone can build**: actions are stacked as readable steps, with variables passed between them.
- **Many ways to start**: app, Siri, Control Center, Action button, Home Screen widget, search, another app, Apple Watch, back tap.
- **Gallery** of ready-made shortcuts and **import questions** that ask the recipient for their own values.
- **Apps offer their actions** to Shortcuts (app shortcuts).
- Listed here for the idea of automation as a first-class feature of the system (ADR 0013).
