## What is a Democracy System?

A democracy system is a platform to support democratic decision-making processes and citizen participation. Democracy systems include:

- Voting and election systems
- Petitions and citizen initiatives
- Discussions and debate platforms
- Proposal submission and evaluation
- Transparency and traceability
- Public accessibility

## Application Areas

- **Corporate democracy**: Employee participation
- **Community and city planning**: Citizen participation in projects
- **Schools and universities**: Student councils and student participation
- **Politics**: Citizen participation in political decisions
- **Open-source projects**: Community decisions

## Benefits of Democracy Systems

- **Transparency**: Traceable decision-making processes
- **Participation**: More citizen participation
- **Acceptance**: Higher acceptance of decisions
- **Efficiency**: Faster decision-making
- **Inclusion**: Inclusion of all stakeholders

## Selection Criteria

When choosing a democracy system, consider the following criteria:

- **Features**: Which features are required?
- **Security**: Voting security and data protection
- **Ease of use**: How user-friendly is it?
- **Scalability**: How many users and decisions?
- **Open Source**: Is the system open source?
- **Integration**: Integration with other tools
