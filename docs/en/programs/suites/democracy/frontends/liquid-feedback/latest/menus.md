# LiquidFeedback - Menu items

## Main navigation

| Menu | Content |
|------|---------|
| Rooms (Areas) | Topic rooms (public or closed), joining rules |
| Initiatives | Ongoing motions: status bars (discussion → quorum → decision) |
| My area | My initiatives, delegation management (to and from me) |
| Delegates | People with delegations, plus voting history |
| Votes | Upcoming and completed votes per room |
| Candidates | Internal party elections (candidate module) |

## Initiative detail (three columns)

- **Proposal** (text, with amendments = "alternative initiatives" as a competing proposal)
- **Arguments** (for/against threads with ratings)
- **Vote** (approval/neutral/rejection, plus delegate flow)

## Administration

| Area | Content |
|------|---------|
| Policy/quorums | Quorum formulas, decision windows, flow principles |
| Users/rooms | Accounts, rights, room types |
| Verification | Sign-up and check chains |

## Menus by role

Member · room admin (committee) · admin; delegates are a role layered on top.
