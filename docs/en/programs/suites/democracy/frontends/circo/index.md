# Circo

> Category: [democracy](../../index.md)

## What it is

Circo (citizen participation and consultation platform, used in Germany and Austria by associations and for parliamentary consultations) combines **surveys, workshops and online consultations** with moderation and result reports — as SaaS or operated by the provider.

> Note: Circo is documented only sparsely; API details are deliberately brief and marked with a status date — no invented endpoints exist here.
