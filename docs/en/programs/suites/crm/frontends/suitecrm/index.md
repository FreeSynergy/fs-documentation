# SuiteCRM Documentation

## Overview

SuiteCRM is an open-source CRM (AGPL-3.0) that emerged in 2013 as a fork of the open SugarCRM suite and is today developed further by the community around SalesAgility. Leads, contacts, companies and opportunities (deals) live in classic CRM modules, which are extended via Studio, workflows and the Module Loader.
