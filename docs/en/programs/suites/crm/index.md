## What is CRM?

CRM (Customer Relationship Management) is a concept for the systematic management of customer relationships. CRM systems include:

- Managing contact data
- Tracking interactions and communication
- Managing the sales pipeline
- Tracking sales opportunities
- Customer analysis and segmentation
- Automating marketing and sales processes

## Application areas

- **Sales**: lead generation and opportunity management
- **Customer service**: customer service and support
- **Marketing**: campaigns and customer segments
- **E-commerce**: customer management for online shops
- **Services**: managing service-provider customers

## Benefits of CRM systems

- **Closeness to the customer**: all customer information in one place
- **Sales support**: identify sales opportunities
- **Customer loyalty**: better customer care
- **Analysis**: customer analysis and reports
- **Automation**: automating processes
- **Integration**: connection to other tools

## Selection criteria

When choosing a CRM system, consider the following criteria:

- **Features**: which features are required?
- **Scalability**: how many customers and contacts?
- **Ease of use**: how easy is it to operate?
- **Integration**: how well does the system fit the existing infrastructure?
- **Cost**: free or paid?
- **Mobile access**: mobile apps available?
