# Odoo

> Category: [erp](../../index.md)

## What it is

Odoo is the open business suite (LGPL core + commercial Enterprise apps): CRM, sales, purchasing, warehouse, manufacturing, accounting, projects, HR — as integrated modules with a shared ORM layer. Python-based, Community Edition self-hosted, Odoo.sh/Cloud optional.
