## What is ERP?

ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) is an integrated software solution for managing all business processes of a company. ERP systems include:

- Financial management
- Procurement and purchasing
- Warehouse management and goods out
- Production and manufacturing
- Sales and marketing
- Human resources
- Customer service

## Application Areas

- **Manufacturing company**: Production and manufacturing
- **Trading company**: Procurement and sales
- **Service providers**: Project management and accounting
- **Commerce**: All commerce industries
- **Public sector**: Administrative ERP

## Benefits of ERP Systems

- **Integration**: All business processes in one place
- **Data consistency**: Unified data foundation
- **Transparency**: Real-time insights into all processes
- **Efficiency**: Automation of processes
- **Reporting**: Extensive reporting functions

## Selection Criteria

When choosing an ERP system, consider the following criteria:

- **Features**: Which modules are required?
- **Scalability**: How many users and locations?
- **Integration**: Integration with existing systems
- **Ease of use**: How user-friendly is it?
- **Costs**: Costs and licensing models
- **Open Source**: Is the system open source?
