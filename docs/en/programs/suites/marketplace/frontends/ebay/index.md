# eBay

> Category: [marketplace](../../index.md)

## What it is

eBay is the global marketplace for C2C/B2C with auction and fixed-price formats. For automation: **Sell API** (listings/orders), **Browse API** (catalog/search), **Finding APIs**, OAuth2 (application ID + user token). Rules/fees/buyer protection come from the operator; dock skins onto them, do not replace them.
